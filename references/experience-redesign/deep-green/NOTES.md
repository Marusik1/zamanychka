# Reference

Deep Green

## URL

- https://deepgreen.app/

## What is valuable

Deep Green is valuable because it treats a board and pieces as the product, not as decoration. It provides the quality bar for material restraint: pieces should feel physical, lit, and calm. The interface should serve the board and avoid noisy visual systems around it.

For Zamanushka, Deep Green defines the quality target for pawns, die, lighting, and selection states.

## Specific screens / interactions inspected

- Public product site and board-first presentation.
- Material language around board pieces and focused gameplay composition.

Screenshot capture was not available in this environment. No screenshot files were fabricated.

## Motion patterns

- Controlled transitions around selected pieces.
- Motion supports understanding of state and available action.
- No casino-like celebration; victory/result should feel earned and physical.
- Board/piece focus remains stable during interaction.

## Layout patterns

- Board-first layout.
- UI chrome is restrained and secondary.
- Piece material and lighting do much of the emotional work.
- Mobile composition should preserve board scale before secondary panels.

## Hover / touch patterns

- Selection states should be visible but not loud.
- Available moves can glow/mark softly.
- A piece should feel pickable through lift, shadow, or subtle scale.

## What Zamanushka should borrow

- Material restraint.
- Painted wood, matte ceramic, or enamel surfaces.
- Board-first mobile composition.
- Quiet selection affordances.
- Result presentation through pieces/lighting rather than confetti.

## What Zamanushka must NOT copy

- Chess-specific identity.
- Minimalism that hides multiplayer room state.
- Glossy luxury/chrome treatment.
- Any exact board/piece design.

## Technical observations

- Piece assets should be compact and reusable.
- Textures should be small, compressed, and shared.
- Lighting direction should be simple and stable on low-power mobile.
- Fallbacks can use high-quality raster/SVG-like CSS presentation when WebGL is unavailable.
